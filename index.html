    Behind the scenes: every phone and laptop reads the same shared database, so a drop posted on one device appears live on the others. Your email is turned into a scrambled ID before anything is saved.

    Privacy notice

    What DropZone is. A feed that belongs to a place. Whoever is physically in a zone can read and add to it. There are no followers and no permanent profiles. Each visit uses a temporary, rotating name.

    Location. Location is opt-in. The app compares one position fix with zone circles in memory and keeps only the zone ID. Coordinates are never saved or shown, and nobody can see where an individual person is. You can leave a zone or deny location at any time, which locks posting.

    How presence is checked. A fix worse than 50 m accuracy is an estimate and gives a read-only view. A position that jumps faster than is physically possible is treated as unverified. You can also scan the rotating QR at a zone entrance. These checks reduce spoofing but do not eliminate it.

    Expiry. Every drop has a timer, and echoes from people in the zone extend it up to a limit. When the timer ends the drop disappears from the live feed. This does not guarantee removal from server logs, backups, or any screenshot someone took before it expired.

    Zone story. Drops that earn two or more echoes, and anonymous mood counts, are kept for 24 hours so a zone can show a recap, then deleted. Poster names are not kept.

    Official announcements. Staff can pin labelled announcements, limited to 2 live per zone.

    Offers and joins. Claiming an offer or joining a group records only your temporary name, and the record disappears with the drop.

    Replies. People verified in a zone can reply to a drop. Replies are screened automatically, limited to 3 per person per drop, and disappear when the drop expires. Outside the zone you see only the reply count.

    Ghost view. If you are not in a zone you can see its mood and the number of live drops, but drop contents and photos stay hidden.

    Moderation. Every drop passes an automatic keyword and scam screen. Flagged drops wait for a human moderator. Any drop can be reported: scam and privacy reports hide it at once, other reports need two different people. After three confirmed violations a fourth removes that identity from posting.

    Email. Used at entry only to hold reporters and posters accountable. It stays on your device. Only a scrambled ID is saved with your drops, and it is never shown or used for marketing.

    Zone summary. The mood summary on each zone is generated on your device from live pulses and drops. Drops and pulses are saved in a shared database so people in the zone see them live.

    Minors. If used in a school or youth event, posting should be limited for users identified as minors and no precise location should ever be shown.

    Sponsored content and emergencies. This prototype has no sponsored posts. Any future sponsored drop must carry a Sponsored label and a frequency cap. DropZone makes no emergency promises and never implies emergency services were contacted.
